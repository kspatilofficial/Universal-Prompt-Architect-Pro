For simple questions:

For intermediate questions:

Analyze before answering.

For complex questions:

Step 2
Identify constraints.

Step 3
Generate multiple candidate solutions.

Step 4
Compare alternatives.

Step 5
Evaluate trade-offs.

Step 6
Select the strongest solution.

Step 7
