# 🤖 AI & Machine Learning Master Knowledge Pack
## Volume 5 — AI Safety, Evaluation & Governance
**Version:** 1.0

# Purpose
Provide foundational guidance for developing, deploying, and using AI systems responsibly while evaluating quality, managing risks, and establishing governance practices.

---

# 1. AI Safety

AI safety focuses on ensuring AI systems behave as intended and minimize the risk of harmful outcomes.

Core objectives:
- Reliability
- Robustness
- Predictability
- Human oversight

---

# 2. Responsible AI Principles

Key principles:
- Fairness
- Transparency
- Accountability
- Privacy
- Security
- Human-centered design

---

# 3. Bias & Fairness

Potential sources of bias:
- Training data
- Prompt wording
- Evaluation methods

Mitigation practices:
- Diverse datasets
- Balanced testing
- Human review
- Continuous monitoring

---

# 4. Privacy & Security

Best practices:
- Protect sensitive data
- Minimize data collection
- Control access
- Follow applicable regulations
- Avoid exposing confidential information

---

# 5. Risk Assessment

Evaluate:
- Likelihood of failure
- Impact of errors
- User safety
- Operational risks
- Legal considerations

Categorize risks as:
- Low
- Medium
- High

---

# 6. Model Evaluation

Evaluate models using:
- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Consistency
- Robustness
- Explainability
- User satisfaction

---

# 7. Benchmarking

Compare models or prompts using standardized tasks.

Benchmark goals:
- Measure quality
- Track improvements
- Identify weaknesses

---

# 8. Hallucination Mitigation

Reduce unsupported outputs by:
- Using grounded sources
- Providing sufficient context
- Requesting citations when appropriate
- Encouraging clarification for missing information
- Validating important responses

---

# 9. Human Oversight

Human review is recommended for:
- Medical information
- Legal information
- Financial decisions
- Safety-critical tasks
- High-impact business decisions

---

# 10. Governance Framework

Governance should define:
- Roles and responsibilities
- Approval workflows
- Documentation
- Audit trails
- Version management
- Continuous monitoring

---

# Continuous Improvement

Lifecycle:
Plan → Build → Test → Deploy → Monitor → Improve
